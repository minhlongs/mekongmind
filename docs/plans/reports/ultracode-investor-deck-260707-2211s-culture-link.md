## Slide 5 — The Product

**Ultracode — two modes:**

**Mode 1: Sprint Battle (now)**
4-minute managed sprint sessions for devs. Drawing → code → silent review → close. Gamified rhythm that builds discipline.

**Mode 2: AI Shadow Review (Q3–Q4)**
Every PR gets automated review inline — defect density, logic gaps, consistency against your codebase's own conventions. Human senior review becomes judgment, not discovery.

**The stack:**
Cloudflare Workers, D1, TypeScript, Inngest, OpenRouter + Anthropic routing.

1. **AI review is table stakes.** Cursor, GitHub Copilot, CodeRabbit — tools exist. The gap is *speed + context*. Review at sprint speed, with codebase-native context. That's what no one has built.

2. **Vietnam engineering talent is a proven output, not a promise.** The Free Fire tournament proved: when Vietnamese engineers show up, they outperform. 55,000 people saw that. Now enterprises need to see the same signal in their pipelines.

3. **SEA enterprise software buying is accelerating.** Regional VCs are deploying. Companies are looking for local-first, cost-efficient alternatives to US-built devtools. Ultracode is building on a stack (Cloudflare Workers) that works on 2G as well as fiber.
