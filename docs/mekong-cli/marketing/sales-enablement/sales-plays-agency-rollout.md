# Sales Play: Agency Rollout

> **Inside Sales Play for Digital Agencies**
> *Convert agencies from hourly billing to outcome-based delivery by rolling out RaaS across their entire project portfolio.*

**Play Type:** Inside Sales | **Track:** Mid-Market | **Owner:** AE Team

## Play Overview

### Objective

Convert **15% of demo'd agencies** to Pro or Enterprise tier by demonstrating multi-client ROI and team-wide productivity gains within 30 days.

### When to Use This Play

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Agency owner requests demo | Trigger Agency Rollout sequence |
| 10+ person dev team | Position as team multiplier |
| Hourly billing model | Position outcomes vs hours |
| Turning down projects | Position capacity unlock |
| Multiple client projects | Position multi-client ROI |

### Expected Metrics

| Metric | Target | Industry Benchmark |
|--------|--------|-------------------|
| Demo Request → Completed | 80% | 65% |

- Blended Rate: $150/hr
- Clients: 12

    = 231x ROI
