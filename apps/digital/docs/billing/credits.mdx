---
title: "Credits"
description: "How credits work"
---

Credits are consumed with each MCP tool call.

## Usage

```bash
mekong credits
```

Shows your current balance, usage this month, and remaining credits.

## Credit Costs

| Tool | Cost |
|------|------|
| Simple (memory_search, task_list) | 1 credit |
| Moderate (plan_create, task_create) | 1 credit |
| Complex (plan_execute, agent_spawn) | 2-3 credits |

## Monthly Reset

Credits reset on the first day of each billing cycle. Unused credits do not roll over.

## Top Up

Pro and Enterprise plans can purchase additional credit packs:

```bash
mekong credits buy 10000
```
