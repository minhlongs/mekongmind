## 2. Barista Service Standards

### 2.1 Customer Greeting
- **Within 30 seconds** of customer entering: Smile, make eye contact, say "Xin chào! Chào mừng đến AURA CAFE!"
- If customer seems undecided: Offer today's special or best-seller recommendation
- Regulars: Greet by name if known, ask about their usual order

### 2.2 Order Taking
1. Confirm order details clearly: repeat back to customer
2. Ask about modifications (sugar level, ice level, hot/iced)
3. Inform customer of wait time estimate
4. Call out order number when ready
5. Verify customer identity when calling order

### 2.3 Drink Preparation Standards
| Drink Type | Standard Time | Quality Check |
|------------|--------------|---------------|
| Espresso-based (hot) | 2-3 min | Crema golden, no bitterness |
| Pour-over | 4-5 min | Clean, no channeling |
| Cold brew / Iced coffee | 2 min | Correct ice level |
| Fresh juice | 1-2 min | No pulp if requested smooth |
| Combo set | 3-4 min | Coffee + pastry together |

### 2.4 Espresso Shot Standards (Single Origin Mekong)
- Dose: 18-20g
- Extraction time: 25-30 seconds
- Volume: 30-40ml (single shot), 60-80ml (double)
- Crema: Thick, hazelnut color, lasting >2 minutes
- Taste profile: Chocolate base, citrus acidity, clean finish
- Temperature: Served at 65-70°C (too hot = burnt, too cold = flat)

### 2.5 Milk Steaming Standards
- Temperature: 60-65°C maximum (scalding point)
- Texture: Microfoam, no large bubbles
- Pour technique: Slow start, finish with latte art (heart/rosetta)
- For cappuccino: More foam, less milk (1:1:1 ratio)
- For latte: Less foam, more milk (1:2:4 ratio)

## 3. Cashier & POS Procedures

### 3.1 Transaction Flow
1. Scan items / select from menu on POS
2. Apply any discounts (loyalty, combo, promotion)
3. Show total to customer
4. Accept payment (cash/MoMo/ZaloPay/bank transfer)
5. Print receipt (always print for cash transactions)
6. Hand receipt to customer with thank you
7. Call out order number

### 3.2 Cash Handling
- Cash drawer target float: 2,000,000 VND (~$80)
- Bills by denomination: organized (500k, 200k, 100k, 50k, 20k, 10k)
- Coins: separate section
- End of day: Count cash, print Z-report, reconcile with POS
- Cash drop procedure: If cash exceeds 5,000,000 VND, deposit to safe
- Cash discrepancy: Report to owner immediately, document in log

### 3.3 Digital Payment Reconciliation
- MoMo: Daily reconciliation via MoMo Merchant app
- ZaloPay: Daily reconciliation via ZaloPay Merchant
- Bank transfer: Match against bank statement next business day
- Delivery platforms (Grab/ShopeeFood): Weekly reconciliation

### 3.4 Refund Policy
- Wrong order: Full refund + remake + apology discount (10%)
- Quality complaint: Refund or remake + compensation (free drink)
- All refunds logged in POS with reason code
- Owner approval required for refunds > 100,000 VND

## 5. Customer Service Standards

### 5.1 Service Philosophy
> "Every cup tells a story. Make theirs a good one."

### 5.2 Customer Interaction Guidelines
- Always smile & make eye contact
- Use "em" or "anh/chị" respectfully based on customer age
- If customer waits >5 minutes, offer apology + small gesture (extra shot, cookie)
- Handle complaints calmly: Listen → Apologize → Resolve → Follow up
- Remember regulars' preferences when possible
- Thank every customer as they leave

### 5.3 Complaint Escalation
| Severity | Example | Action |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Low | Wrong order, minor delay | Barista handles directly |
| Medium | Cold drink, unsatisfactory quality | Barista + Manager |
| High | Health concern, hygiene issue | Owner immediately |
| Critical | Customer injury, serious complaint | Owner + immediate documentation |

### 5.4 Special Situations
- **VIP customers (influencers, regulars)**: Priority seating, free upgrade offer
- **Large groups (6+)**: Pre-notify barista, prepare in batches
- **Phone orders**: Confirm order, pickup time, payment method
- **Delivery platform orders**: Priority queue, separate preparation area
- **Allergies**: Always ask about allergies, label clearly, maintain allergen-free prep zone

## 7. Emergency Procedures

### 7.1 Power Outage
1. Stay calm, do not panic customers
2. Save current orders on paper if POS down
3. Use cash-only mode temporarily
4. Contact utility company if extended outage
5. If equipment damage suspected → do not restart until inspected

### 7.2 Equipment Failure
| Equipment | Backup Plan | Owner Contact |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Espresso machine | Manual pour-over only | Coffee equipment service |
| Grinder | Pre-ground coffee (1 day supply) | Same |
| POS system | Paper receipts, manual cash | POS vendor |
| AC failure | Fans, early closing if hot | AC service |
| Refrigerator | Immediate: ice bath for milk | Refrigeration service |

### 7.3 Health / Safety Incident
- Customer allergic reaction: Call emergency (115), provide antihistamine if available, do not move customer
- Slip/fall: Provide first aid, document incident, check camera footage
- Food contamination: Remove product immediately, report to owner, do not serve

### 7.4 Security
- If suspicious activity: Do not confront, call police (113), lock door
- If robbery: Comply with demands, call police after, document everything
- Camera footage: Review daily, archive weekly
