# AURA CAFE — Company Blueprint
## F&B Container Cafe | Sa Đéc, Đồng Tháp, Vietnam

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**AURA CAFE** is a premium container-based coffee & beverage cafe located in Sa Đéc city, Đồng Tháp province, Mekong Delta, Vietnam. Operating from a repurposed shipping container, AURA CAFE delivers specialty coffee, fresh juices, and light meals to the local community with a modern, Instagram-worthy aesthetic.

- **Launch Date**: 06/06/2026
- **Location**: Sa Đéc, Đồng Tháp, Vietnam
- **Format**: Shipping container cafe (~20ft, ~30 seats indoor + outdoor)
- **Business Model**: Dine-in + Takeaway + Delivery (Grab/ShopeeFood)
- **Target Revenue (Month 1)**: 63,000,000 VND (~$2,500 USD)
- **Target Revenue (Year 1)**: 500,000,000 VND (~$20,000 USD/month)

## 2. Market & Location Analysis

### 2.1 Target Market
| Segment | Profile | Share |
|---------|---------|-------|
| Students | 18-25, nearby universities | 40% |
| Young Professionals | 25-35, office workers | 30% |
| Tourists | Domestic Mekong Delta visitors | 15% |
| Families | Weekend/holiday visitors | 15% |

### 2.2 Competitive Landscape
- **Direct competitors**: Local traditional coffee shops, Highlands Coffee nearby
- **Differentiation**: Container aesthetic, specialty coffee, Instagram-worthy space, lower prices
- **Market gap**: Modern aesthetic + quality coffee at accessible prices in Sa Đéc

### 2.3 Location Rationale
- Sa Đéc: Growing city, tourist destination (flower village nearby)
- High foot traffic area near residential + commercial zones
- Container format: Low setup cost, unique brand identity, fast deployment

## 9. Scale Pathway (Road to $1M MRR)

```
Phase 1: Single Container (Now - Month 6)
  → $2,100 - $4,800 MRR
  → Prove unit economics, build brand

Phase 2: Multi-Location Expansion (Month 7 - Month 18)
  → 5 containers across Đồng Tháp & neighboring provinces
  → $10,000 - $25,000 MRR
  → Standardize operations, franchise model

Phase 3: Regional Network (Month 19 - Month 36)
  → 20 containers across Mekong Delta
  → $42,000 - $85,000 MRR
  → Franchise + supply chain hub

Phase 4: National Brand (Year 3+)
  → 50-100 locations nationwide
  → $150,000 - $350,000 MRR
  → Brand recognition, digital platform

Phase 5: Platform + Franchise (Year 4+)
  → $1,000,000 MRR
  → Franchise fees + supply chain + digital ordering platform
```
