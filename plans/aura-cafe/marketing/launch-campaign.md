## Grand Opening: 06/06/2026 | Sa Đéc, Đồng Tháp

## 1. Brand Identity

### 1.1 Brand Positioning
**Tagline**: "Cà phê trong container — Không gian hiện đại giữa lòng Sa Đéc"
(Coffee in a container — Modern space in the heart of Sa Đéc)

### 1.2 Visual Identity
- **Primary color**: Warm wood tones (#8B4513) + white minimalist
- **Secondary color**: Sage green (#87A878) for freshness
- **Logo**: Modern minimal "AC" monogram with container silhouette
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif (Montserrat for headings, Open Sans for body)
- **Aesthetic**: Industrial chic — concrete, wood, greenery, warm lighting

### 1.3 Brand Voice
- Tone: Warm, approachable, slightly playful
- Language: Vietnamese (primary), English (secondary)
- Key messages: Quality coffee, unique space, community hub, affordable luxury

## 6. Competitive Positioning

### 6.1 Competitive Advantages
| Advantage | AURA CAFE | Competitors |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Unique space (container) | ✅ | ❌ |
| Specialty coffee (pour-over, single origin) | ✅ | Limited |
| Instagram-worthy aesthetic | ✅ | Limited |
| Competitive pricing | ✅ | Mixed |
| Digital-first ordering | ✅ | Mixed |
| Community hub events | ✅ | Limited |
| Local Mekong Delta sourcing | ✅ | Mixed |

### 6.2 Competitive Response Plan
- If competitor lowers prices: Emphasize quality, not price war
- If new cafe opens nearby: Accelerate loyalty program, deepen community ties
- If delivery fees change: Optimize own delivery, bundle offers
