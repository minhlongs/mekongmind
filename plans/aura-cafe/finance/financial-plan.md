## 6. Tax & Compliance

### 6.1 Business Registration
- **Entity type**: Hộ kinh doanh cá thể (Sole Proprietorship) or Công ty TNHH (LLC)
- **Tax code**: Apply at Đồng Tháp Provincial Tax Department
- **VAT**: 0% (below VAT threshold) or 10% if registered
- **Business license**: From Sa Đéc City People's Committee
- **Food safety**: Certificate from Đồng Tháp Department of Health
